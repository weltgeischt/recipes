Pure Maltodextrin Gel
###########

Objective
**********

A concentrated and low-viscosity gel for the fructose intolerant and those who don't like it overly sweet.
Avoid GI issues associated with sodium bicarbonate.


Ingredients
**********

* 500 g maltodextrin
* 300 ml water (preferably lukewarm)
* salt according to individual needs, e.g. 8g sodium citrate, 1g potassium citrate
* optional: sugar- and sweetener-free flavor drops, or just lemon juice


Directions
**********

* put water into a bowl, add flavoring (if using)
* whisk in maltodextrin and salt slowly to avoid clumps
* storage: put in jam jars and keep in fridge or freezer

Usage
**********

sip from soft-flask and wash down with water

Nutrition facts
**********

* recipe results in ~670 ml gel
* (0.64 g carbs)/(g gel)
* (0.75 g carbs)/(ml gel)
**Comparison**: GU gels have ~ (0.7g carbs)/(g gel)


Enjoy!
